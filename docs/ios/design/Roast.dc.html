The Basement

Your Moon isn’t in Scorpio so much as hiding from the police there. The 4th house is home, family, the foundations, and you have put a single bare bulb down there and a chair facing the wall.

Mars squares it at less than a degree, so every feeling arrives already armed. You don’t hold grudges. You curate them. Climate-controlled, labelled, a small gift shop on the way out.
